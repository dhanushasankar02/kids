Quick Answers

The four questions we hear most often — answered in seconds.

01

How do I enroll my child?

Send us a message or call us — we'll guide you through a free assessment and match your child to the perfect program.

02

Can we tour the pool first?

Absolutely. Drop by any location during office hours or schedule a guided tour with one of our coaches.

03

What if my child is nervous?

That's exactly what we specialize in. Our coaches are trained in gentle, trust-first teaching methods.

Yes — your first trial lesson is always free. No commitment, no pressure, just a happy splash.
