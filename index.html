The Confidence Journey

Four gentle milestones that take your child from first splash to fearless swimmer.

1

First Splash

Gentle water introduction with songs, toys, and zero pressure. We let curiosity lead the way while parents stay poolside.

Ages 2–3 Parent & Me
2

Float & Breathe

Kids master the star float and rhythmic breathing — the two superhero skills that keep them calm and safe in any water.

Ages 4–5 Safety Skills
3

Kick & Glide

Armed with floats and lots of cheers, kids learn to propel themselves forward — the thrilling moment independence clicks.

Ages 5–7 Movement
4

Stroke Ready

Freestyle, backstroke, and water safety drills come together. Your child now swims with real confidence and technique.

Our Safety Pledge to Every Family

Safety isn't a feature — it's the foundation. Here's what we promise before your child even touches the water:

  • CPR & First-Aid certified coaches on deck at all times
  • Maximum 6 students per coach — never a crowded lane
  • Daily water quality tests logged and publicly visible
  • Emergency action plan rehearsed weekly by all staff
