How We Train Our Coaches

Becoming an AquaKids coach is just the beginning. Continuous development keeps our team at the top of their game.

Stage 1 · Weeks 1–4

Foundation Training

Intensive onboarding covering our curriculum, safety protocols, and child psychology fundamentals.

Stage 2 · Months 2–3

Mentored Teaching

New coaches shadow senior instructors, co-teach classes, and receive weekly feedback sessions.

Stage 3 · Ongoing

Video Review & Peer Coaching

Monthly video reviews of lessons, plus peer coaching circles where instructors share best practices.

Stage 4 · Annually

Advanced Certification

Yearly advanced training in specialty areas — toddler development, competitive coaching, or adaptive swim.
