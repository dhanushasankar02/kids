Safety Is Not Optional

Water Safety Starts
Before the First Splash

Drowning is the #1 cause of accidental death in children under 5. Knowledge, vigilance, and preparation turn every swim into a safe adventure.

See Our Swim Programs

The 5 Golden Rules of Water Safety

Simple, memorable rules that every parent and child should know by heart before entering any water.

01

Never Swim Alone

Always have a designated adult watching — not distracted.

02

Reach, Don't Jump

Use a pole or throw a float — never jump in after someone.

03

Check the Water

Know the depth, temperature, and current before entering.

04

Learn to Float

Floating is the first survival skill — teach it early.

05

Know Emergency Steps

Call for help, don't panic, and follow the rescue plan.

Know the Hidden Dangers

Water looks harmless — but it hides risks that every parent should recognize. Here are the danger zones we teach families to spot instantly.

  • Silent Drowning Children rarely splash or shout — they slip under quietly.
  • Pool Drains & Suction Strong suction can trap hair or limbs — teach kids to avoid them.
  • Cold Water Shock Sudden cold can cause gasping and panic — enter slowly.
  • Slippery Pool Decks Most injuries happen on wet tiles — always walk, never run.

If Something Goes Wrong

Three critical rescue steps every parent should know — calm, clear, and safe for everyone.

01

Shout & Point

Call out clearly to alert others, and point to the exact location in the water. Keep your eyes locked on the person at all times.

Alert Others
02

Reach or Throw

Use a rescue pole, rope, or floatation device. Never enter the water yourself unless you are trained — many rescuers become victims.

Safe Rescue
03

Call for Help

Dial emergency services immediately. If CPR is needed and you're trained, begin compressions. Stay on the line until help arrives.

Emergency Call
