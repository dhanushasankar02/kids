Freestyle vs Backstroke

Two foundational strokes, compared side-by-side. Understanding the difference helps kids master both faster.

Freestyle

Speed & Power

  • High-elbow catch for maximum pull
  • Bilateral breathing rhythm
  • Hip-driven rotation
  • Flutter kick with pointed toes
VS
Backstroke

Balance & Control

  • Straight-arm recovery over water
  • Continuous flutter kick
  • Body line awareness
  • Rhythmic arm alternation

Every Stroke, Broken Down

We don't just teach strokes — we teach the physics behind them. Kids learn why each movement works, not just how to do it.

01

Body Position

Streamlined, horizontal, and relaxed. The foundation every stroke is built on.

02

Breathing Rhythm

Timing the breath to the stroke — the skill that separates beginners from swimmers.

03

Arm Recovery

Over-water or underwater — each stroke has its own recovery path.

04

Kick Timing

From flutter to dolphin — the kick sets the rhythm for the entire stroke.

05

Rotation & Core

Hip and shoulder rotation generate power without wasting energy.

06

Flip Turns

The advanced skill that turns laps into seamless, continuous swimming.
