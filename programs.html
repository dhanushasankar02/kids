Your Child's Learning Path

Five progressive stages that take a child from first splash to confident, capable swimmer.

1

Water Discovery

Getting comfortable with water on the face, entering safely, and trusting the coach.

Beginner 4–6 weeks
2

Float & Breathe

Mastering front and back floats, rhythmic breathing, and safe recovery positions.

Foundation 6–8 weeks
3

Move & Glide

Kicking, gliding, and the first independent movements across the pool.

Intermediate 8–10 weeks
4

Stroke Introduction (Junior Strokes)

Learning freestyle and backstroke with proper arm and leg coordination.

Advanced 10–12 weeks
5

Stroke Refinement

Building endurance, refining technique, and preparing for competitive swimming.

Mastery 12+ weeks

Program Questions, Answered

Everything parents ask before enrolling their child in a swim program.

How do I know which program is right for my child?

We recommend starting with a free 15-minute assessment. Our coaches evaluate your child's comfort level, current skills, and learning style — then place them in the perfect group.

What is the class size for group programs?

Group classes have a maximum of 6 students per coach. This ensures every child gets personal attention and stays safe at all times.

Can my child switch programs mid-session?

Absolutely. If your child progresses faster (or needs more time), we'll move them to the right group at no extra cost. Progress is always the priority.

What should my child bring to lessons?

A well-fitted swimsuit, goggles, a towel, and a swim cap (optional). We provide all floatation aids and training equipment at the pool.

Do you offer makeup classes for missed sessions?

Yes — with at least 24 hours' notice, you can schedule a makeup class within the same month, subject to availability. No-shows without notice cannot be rescheduled.
