Seasonal Program Calendar

Our program cycles align with the seasons — each season brings new skills, new badges, and new celebrations.

Spring Session

Mar – May

Building water confidence after the winter break. Focus on floating and breathing.

Summer Session

Jun – Aug

Intensive stroke development with daily options. Perfect for rapid progress.

Fall Session

Sep – Nov

Endurance building and stroke refinement. Introduction to competitive skills.

Winter Session

Dec – Feb

Indoor pool mastery. Maintaining skills and preparing for the next season.
