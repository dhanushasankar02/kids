Water Safety Rules & Emergency Protocols

Essential safety guidelines, pool deck behavior, floating survival, and CPR action steps.

Golden Pool Rules

Core rules every young swimmer must master before entering the deep pool area.

  • Never swim without adult or coach supervision
  • Always walk on pool decks (No running)
  • Look before diving & obey depth markers
  • Practice the Buddy System at all times

Floating & Survival Skills

Techniques taught to stay calm and conserve energy during unexpected water entries.

  • Starfish Back Float for effortless breathing
  • Treading water with eggbeater kick
  • Roll-to-float breathing orientation
  • Safe wall grab & climb out technique

Emergency CPR & Action Steps

Immediate steps for parents and coaches in case of water emergency.

  • Reach or Throw, Don't Go (Use rescue tube)
  • Call Emergency Services (911/Local) immediately
  • Begin CPR: 30 Chest compressions to 2 rescue breaths
  • Ensure AED is retrieved and attached

Stroke Mechanics & Technique

Detailed technique breakdowns for freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly.

Freestyle (Front Crawl)

High elbow catch, side-breathing rhythm every 3 strokes, and flutter kick speed.

Backstroke Alignment

Keep chin tilted up, hips elevated near water surface, and straight arm pinky entry.

Breaststroke Timing

Pull, breathe, kick, glide sequence with whip kick ankle rotation.

Bubble Blowing Games

Starfish Floats
